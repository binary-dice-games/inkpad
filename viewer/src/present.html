Present with inksplat

Share a browser tab, a window or a whole screen, even a desktop application. Viewers open a link, watch it live and draw on it with you. Nothing to install.

Or start a shared board with no video, to draw, write and share files together.

Everything in what you choose will be visible to anyone with the link. You choose in the next step, and your browser always shows when you are sharing.

You draw on a copy of what you share, in this window, so keep this window next to it: anything drawn on the shared screen itself would not reach viewers.
